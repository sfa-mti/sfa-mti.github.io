03

Problem

When spending is spread across several accounts, checking each banking app does not always make it easy to see the total or compare it with a monthly budget.

01

Spending across accounts

Total spending, recurring payments and budget progress can be difficult to review together.

02

Manual tracking

Collecting and categorizing data takes time before users can evaluate their spending.

03

Data privacy

Users need to understand what financial information they share and how it will be processed.

Class discussion. When we presented the idea, classmates said they used multiple accounts and would find a combined view useful. We still need interviews to understand how often this causes a problem and what they do about it now.
04

Solution and value proposition

We plan to bring transactions from supported bank accounts into SFA, group expenses by category and compare them with the user’s budget. Recommendations will explain which spending patterns may be worth reviewing.

The aim is to reduce the work needed to review spending across accounts and decide where to adjust a budget.
Existing baseline

The thesis prototype

  • Bank-statement import and categorization
  • Dashboard, budgets and alerts
  • Recurring-transaction detection
  • Rule-based recommendations
  • AI-generated monthly summaries
Planned development

The team project

  • Consent-based, read-only Open Banking integration
  • A consolidated onboarding experience
  • Recommendations linked to supporting data
  • Review of users' chosen budget adjustments

We have not selected an Open Banking provider yet. We need to check bank coverage and costs, explain what data is shared, and implement access revocation and deletion. We also want to limit the information sent to external AI services.

05

Customer segments

Primary segment

People in Romania who actively use at least two bank accounts.

They want to manage their spending without repeatedly consolidating their finances by hand.

Initial recruitment

We plan to start with students who have an income and people in their first years of work, using university and professional contacts to find interview participants.

We are starting with individual budgets. We may explore household budgeting later if interviews show a need for it.

07

Proposed competitive advantage

We want users to be able to check why SFA makes a recommendation, choose whether to follow it and review the result later.

  1. Identify

    Find a spending change or a budget that needs attention.

  2. Explain

    Show the period, transactions and calculation behind the suggestion.

  3. Choose

    Let the user correct the data, reject the suggestion or choose an adjustment.

  4. Review

    Compare later spending with the adjustment the user chose.

We need to find out whether this is more useful than the tools people already use. Competitors also offer bank connections and AI features, so those alone are not enough to set SFA apart.
08

Key metrics

These are the metrics we plan to track when we have pilot users.

MetricWhat we will measure
ActivationShare of eligible onboarding starters who connect two accounts and view their consolidated overview.
Time to first useful insightTime from onboarding start to a recommendation the user understands and reports finding useful.
Week-four retentionShare of activated users who review their finances or update a budget during the fourth week after activation.
Recommendation usefulnessHelpful ratings divided by all recommendation ratings, reported together with the number of ratings.
Data reliabilitySynchronization success and transaction/category accuracy on a verified sample, including duplicate and internal-transfer checks.
Cost per active userMonthly operating costs divided by monthly active users, tracking bank-provider and AI costs separately.

We will use the first pilot results to set realistic targets.

09

Cost structure

Baseline

Infrastructure

Application hosting, database, domain, monitoring and backups.

Usage-dependent

Connected services

Bank-data access, AI requests, storage growth and payment processing. Provider minimum commitments may add fixed costs.

Ongoing

People & maintenance

Development, support, integration upkeep, security and privacy work, and user research.

We will estimate monthly costs once we know the provider’s terms and expected usage. Development time also counts as a project cost.
